Policies & Legal

Privacy Policy

We collect information you provide directly (name, email, phone) when you book sessions or contact us. We use standard security measures to protect this data. We do not share your information with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.

Use of Information

Your information is used to provide services, respond to inquiries, send confirmations, and improve our offerings. We retain your data only as long as necessary for service delivery and legal compliance.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information by contacting us at info@doravitta.com.

All sessions are delivered online via Zoom or Google Meet. You agree to provide accurate contact information and communicate changes promptly. Sessions are confidential and professional in nature.

User Responsibilities

You agree to participate respectfully and professionally. Abusive, discriminatory, or illegal conduct will result in immediate session termination and forfeiture of payment.

Limitation of Liability

DoraVitta provides services "as is." We are not liable for technical issues beyond our control, indirect damages, or losses arising from service use.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

We want you to be satisfied with our services.

Cancellation Terms

Cancellations made 48 hours or more before a scheduled session are eligible for a full refund or credit toward a future session. Cancellations within 48 hours are non-refundable but may be rescheduled for a future date.

No-Show Policy

If you miss a scheduled session without prior notice, payment is forfeited and cannot be refunded or credited.

Satisfaction Guarantee

If you are unsatisfied with your first session, we will work with you to reschedule or provide guidance. Contact us within 7 days of your session.

Our services do not substitute for professional medical, psychological, or legal advice. If you need mental health support, please contact a licensed therapist or counselor.

We reserve the right to discontinue services due to technical issues, circumstances beyond our control, or policy violations. We will provide reasonable notice when possible.

Payment Policy

All payments are handled securely online.

Payment Methods

We accept payment by card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) via a secure payment processor. A payment link is sent after your session is booked.

Security

Your payment information is encrypted and processed securely. We do not store credit card details. All transactions comply with PCI DSS standards.

Billing

Payment is due upon booking. You will receive a confirmation email with your session details and receipt. Refunds (if applicable) are processed to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.

Disputed Charges

If you have questions about a charge, contact us at support@doravitta.com before disputing the charge with your bank.
