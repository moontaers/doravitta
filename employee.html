Employee Coaching & Motivation | DoraVitta LLC

Practical coaching sessions to build confidence, motivation, and useful habits for handling work pressure.

What sessions cover

We focus on goal setting, communication, motivation, confidence, and practical habits that help with workplace pressure.

Who it’s for

Individual employees looking for practical support, and teams seeking a shared coaching and motivation plan.

How it works

  1. Contact us at info@doravitta.com to discuss your needs.
  2. Agree on a session plan and confirm a schedule.
  3. Meet online for the agreed coaching sessions.

Pricing and payment

Individual session: $55 for 45 minutes. Team pricing is available on request. Secure online card payment; your payment link is sent after booking.

Important notice

This service provides coaching and motivational support only. It is not medical care, psychotherapy, or a substitute for care from a licensed health professional.
