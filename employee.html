Employee Wellbeing & Coaching

Support for employee wellbeing, recovery and coaching.

Service overview

Our Employee Wellbeing & Coaching programmes provide confidential support for stress, burnout, workplace challenges and personal difficulties. Sessions combine practical coaching with appropriate referrals when specialist support is needed.

How we work

We provide confidential, practical support. Managers can make referrals, or employees can contact us directly. Each programme is tailored to the employee’s needs and the organisation’s goals.
