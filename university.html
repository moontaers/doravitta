University Consultations

One-to-one sessions that help students and families plan their university journey with confidence. We cover choosing a major and university, building a realistic application plan, and preparing for admissions requirements and deadlines.

Who it's for

High school students, current university students, and parents.

How it works

  1. Book a session through the contact form or by email.
  2. We confirm your time and send a secure payment link.
  3. Meet online (Zoom or Google Meet) for 45 minutes.
  4. Receive a short written summary with clear next steps.

Pricing

$15 per session.

Payment

Secure online payment by card. A payment link is sent after booking.
