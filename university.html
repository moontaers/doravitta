University Consultations | DoraVitta LLC

One-to-one sessions to help students and families plan a university journey with clarity and confidence.

What the sessions cover

We help you explore majors and universities, understand admissions requirements, and build a realistic application plan based on your goals and circumstances.

Who it’s for

High school students preparing applications, current university students considering their next steps, and parents supporting a student’s decisions.

How it works

  1. Book by emailing info@doravitta.com.
  2. We confirm a time and send a secure payment link.
  3. Meet online for 45 minutes via Zoom or Google Meet.
  4. Receive a written summary with practical next steps.

Pricing and payment

$15 per 45-minute session. Secure online card payment; your payment link is sent after booking.
